How it works

  1. Add your schedule

    Sign in with your Onyen and enter the McColl classes you need to get to.

  2. Get matched

    We pair you with a student driving to McColl from near your last class, timed to the passing period.

  3. Ride and arrive early

    Hop in, chip in for gas, and walk into class before it starts.

Built for

  • Students coming from North Campus classes
  • Bus riders whose route doesn't line up
  • Students with injuries or disabilities
  • Students heading to evening events
  • Students who drive and have open seats
  • Professors tired of late arrivals

Take a Tar Heel is a BUSI 200 recitation project at UNC Kenan-Flagler.

This prototype uses sample rides and sends no messages. It isn't affiliated with UNC Transportation and Parking or Chapel Hill Transit. Drive times are estimates.
