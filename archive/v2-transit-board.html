Late to McColl? Take a Tar Heel.

You get 15 minutes between classes. The walk from North Campus to the business school takes about 20, and the bus doesn't line up with the bell. Take a Tar Heel puts you in the passenger seat of a student who's already driving there.

      Ride smart

      Rules every rider and driver agrees to.

      • Students onlyEvery account is a UNC student. The real version checks your Onyen before you can ride or drive.
      • Public pickupsRides start at the stops listed here, never at someone's dorm room or apartment.
      • No numbers on the boardRequests and confirmations happen in the app. Nobody's phone number is posted.
      • The driver confirmsA seat is yours once the driver accepts. If they don't, the waitlist shows drivers you still need a ride.
      • Chip in, don't payRiders can offer gas money. Drivers aren't running a taxi and can't charge a fare.

      How it works

      1. Pick your class

        Choose the days and start time of the McColl class you keep running late to, and where your last class lets out.

      2. Request a seat

        Every ride on the board arrives before your class starts. Tap a driver near you; they get your request right away.

      3. Ride and arrive early

        Once they confirm, meet at the pickup stop, hop in and walk into class before it starts.

      Built for

      • Students coming from North Campus classes
      • Bus riders whose route doesn't line up
      • Students with injuries or disabilities
      • Students heading to evening events
      • Students who drive and have open seats
      • Professors tired of late arrivals
      • Students avoiding 8 a.m. classes they can't reach
