    Safety

    Built for students, by students

    • UNC students onlyYou sign in with your UNC email, and the site only accepts UNC addresses.
    • Real profilesEvery driver and rider has a photo, year and bio, so you know who you're meeting.
    • Public pickupsRides start at listed campus spots, never at someone's room or apartment.
    • No phone numbers postedRequests and confirmations happen in the app.
    Ratings

    Two-way ratings

    1 to 5

    After a confirmed ride, riders rate their driver and drivers rate each rider. Tap a few tags like "On time" or "Safe driver" to say why.

    Average ratings show next to every name and on every profile. You can filter to drivers rated 4.5 and up, and drivers see a rider's rating before accepting.

    Questions

    Who can drive?

    UNC juniors, seniors and graduate students with a valid driver's license, their own insurance and a car in good shape. Drivers post rides they're already making.

    How much does a ride cost?

    The driver sets the price, from free up to $5 a seat. Most campus rides are about $3. You pay the driver after the ride by Venmo, Zelle or cash.

    Why add my schedule?

    Once your classes are saved, My schedule shows the rides that line up with each one, and organizers can match you with drivers who have classes at the same time.

    What if nobody is driving at my time?

    Join the waitlist for that time and drop-off. Drivers see how many riders are waiting when they create a ride.

    Who sees my profile?

    Only signed-in UNC students see your photo, name, year, program and bio, on the rides you post or join. Your schedule, email and survey comments are visible to you and the organizers only.

    Is this run by UNC?

    No. Take A Tar Heel is a student project and isn't affiliated with UNC, UNC Transportation and Parking or Chapel Hill Transit.
